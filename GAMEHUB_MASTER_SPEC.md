# Vision

JB³ GameHub is an open-source Minecraft management platform designed to make multiplayer accessible to everyone.

The goal is to remove every technical barrier involved in creating, hosting, managing and enjoying Minecraft servers.

Instead of editing configuration files, downloading plugins manually, forwarding ports or troubleshooting compatibility issues, users interact with a modern dashboard assisted by AI.

GameHub is intended for:

• Families
• Schools
• Content Creators
• Communities
• Small Businesses
• Public Servers
• Minecraft Beginners
• Advanced Administrators

The software should be powerful enough for professionals while remaining simple enough for a parent with no technical knowledge.
