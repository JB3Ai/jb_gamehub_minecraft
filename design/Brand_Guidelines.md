# Brand Guidelines - JB³ GameHub

**Brand Name**: JB³ GameHub  
**Tagline**: Make Minecraft Multiplayer Effortless. One Click. One Dashboard. Every Device.  
**Author**: Jonathan Blackburn (JB³Ai)  

---

## 1. Brand Identity & Mission
JB³ GameHub is designed to be approachable, modern, and reliable. It bridges the gap between deep technical server administration and simple family/creator gaming.

### Core Brand Values
- **Approachable**: Friendly to parents and kids without feeling child-like.
- **Powerful**: Trusted by experienced Minecraft sysadmins and server owners.
- **Modern**: Clean dark mode aesthetics, high contrast, smooth animations, zero clunky legacy UI.
- **Agentic**: AI-assisted management that removes technical complexity effortlessly.

---

## 2. Visual Identity & Logos
- **Primary Mark**: High-tech emerald cube (`#10b981`) combined with sleek terminal/bot typography.
- **Color Accent**: Deep gaming dark canvas (`#09090b`) with vibrant emerald (`#10b981`), bedrock blue (`#3b82f6`), and plugin purple (`#a855f7`).

---

## 3. Tone of Voice
- **Clear & Concise**: Use direct action verbs ("Deploy Server", "Restore Snapshot", "Install Geyser").
- **Jargon-Free Defaults**: Explain technical terms inline or handle them automatically via JB AI Copilot.
- **Empathetic & Helpful**: Friendly error messages with 1-click quick-fix suggestions.
